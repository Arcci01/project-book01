Ang Mutya ng Section E, commonly abbreviated as AMNSE, is a massively popular Philippine teen romantic comedy and drama franchise based on the hit Wattpad books by Lara Flores, who writes under the pseudonym Eatmore2behappy. The story follows Jasper Jean "Jay Jay" Mariano, a girl with a troubled academic record who transfers to the Higher Value International School. Due to her past, she is placed in the notoriously chaotic and all-boys "Section E," becoming the class's only female student and its literal mutya (muse). The plot centers around a classic enemies-to-lovers romance filled with family obstacles, school politics, and an intense love triangle involving the class president, Mark Keifer Watson, and his friend, Yuri Hanamichi. Originally a digital novel that earned millions of reads, AMNSE exploded into a global cultural phenomenon following its live-action television series adaptation on the Viva One streaming platform, starring Andres Muhlach, Ashtine Olviga, and Rabin Angeles.
